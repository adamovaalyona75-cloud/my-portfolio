Selected work

Halo

Fintech · Mobile onboarding · 2024

Halo's signup funnel lost nearly half of new users before they reached their first transfer. I led the redesign of onboarding end to end — simplifying identity verification, rewriting the copy, and restructuring the flow around a single task per screen.

  • −42%drop-off in onboarding
  • 2.1×faster time to first transfer

Meridian

B2B SaaS · Operations dashboard · 2023

Meridian's ops team worked across six disconnected tools to track shipments. I designed a single dashboard that unified their data sources, with a permissions model flexible enough for warehouses, dispatch, and finance to share one screen.

Fieldwork

Design system · Logistics platform · 2023

About

I've spent the last seven years designing products for teams whose work is invisible until something breaks — payments, logistics, internal ops. I care most about the parts of an interface nobody notices: the state that loads correctly, the error that explains itself, the form that doesn't ask twice. Before product design I trained as an industrial designer, which is probably why I still think in systems and tolerances rather than screens.
